Show cumulative TLS and metadata-download diagnostics for one site.
Usage: <cmd> SITENAME

<cmd> CSP

Counters are kept in memory from site creation until removal or process exit.
Reading them does not reset them. window_ms is the age of the counters, not
the duration of the last release. Take two snapshots to compare a time window.
Rows: login (TLS negotiation only), sfv, nfo and other metadata downloads.

hs: completed handshake calls, including failures
ok/err/timeout/cancelled: mutually exclusive handshake outcomes; their sum is hs
hs_ms/hs_max_ms: sum/maximum wall time of handshake calls, including failure cleanup
dl/dl_ok/dl_err: admitted Leechfile calls and their outcomes
dl_ms/dl_max_ms: sum/maximum whole Leechfile time (includes TLS and FTP commands)
retry_err: enqueued SFV/NFO retries after a failed download
retry_wait: enqueued retries because metadata is not ready or capacity is unavailable

Durations use a monotonic clock. Concurrent operations overlap: sums are not
process runtime, and dl_ms must not be added to hs_ms. Totals include failed calls.
Downloads rejected before reserving a slot are not counted as dl.
Retries that have not started yet are counted; retries exhausted without a new
task are not. These totals do not describe direct server-to-server FXP traffic.

session_reuse=disabled identifies this comparison build: no cached sessions are
offered. It is not a measured reuse rate. No additional OpenSSL library is loaded.
Handshake event waiting and SSL_pending reads remain enabled.

Output is on demand only, to the requesting IRC channel or console. Existing
TLS DATA READ messages still report per-release metadata reads separately.
