Writes a self-contained diagnostic report (watchdog.<timestamp>.log next to the binary) containing
heartbeats of all monitored threads, the state of every lock, the kernel state of all threads,
application inventory and the last log lines. Use it when the bot appears stuck, and send the
generated file for analysis. The watchdog also writes such a report automatically when it detects
a stall (see [watchdog] section in slftp.ini).
